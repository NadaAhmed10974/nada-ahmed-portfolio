Who am I

Your data knows where you’re losing money. I’m Nada Ahmed, an AI engineer and a student at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Zagazig National University, working in Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Computer Vision. I turn raw business data into models that lift sales and expose hidden losses.

Whether you’re a local business owner who wants to hit higher sales targets, or a research team with a dataset that needs preparing and modeling, I can take it from raw data to a clear answer.

My work covers complete machine learning models, data processing, model tuning, and computer vision. I completed the AI track at the NTI / Huawei Egyptian Talent Academy (80 hours, 96.5%), and I build real projects: Hard Hat Detection with YOLOv8, a Kepler exoplanet classifier, and a NASA Space Apps project.

I learn by doing, so I join hands-on programs and competitions, and I enjoy working as part of a team. I want to keep growing my skills where the work is real.

What matters to me is a clear answer. When I work with a business owner or a research team, I want you to leave knowing what your data says and what to do next, in plain language.

Have a dataset or project in mind? Send me the details and let’s find the best way to approach it.
